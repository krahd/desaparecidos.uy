## generative-facial-completion

Status: `refused`  
Access: `public`  
Scope: `todos-somos-familiares`, `estan-en-todas-partes`, `seguimos-buscando`

**Available operation:** Inferred completion of unknown facial detail

**Relation refused:** Synthetic appearance presented as recovery, restoration, or simulated return

Decision basis:

- Purpose: Memorial reappearance without simulated return or technological closure
- Mandate: Independent artist-researcher; no forensic, judicial, archival, or organisational authority
- Evidence: The documented portrait remains the historical referent and visible fragments remain materially incomplete

Residual risks:

- A composite may still be mistaken for restoration
- Source selection may reproduce archival exclusions

Review triggers:

- A materially different institutional mandate
- Documented participation by people with standing
- A changed artistic purpose

## biometric-identification

Status: `refused`  
Access: `public`  
Scope: `todos-somos-familiares`, `estan-en-todas-partes`, `seguimos-buscando`

**Available operation:** Face recognition, face-to-name matching, or identity-seeking analysis

**Relation refused:** Living source persons or disappeared persons made into computational identification targets

Decision basis:

- Purpose: Construct memorial appearances through fragments without identifying source persons
- Mandate: The artwork does not hold investigative, forensic, or biometric authority
- Evidence: Reviewed provenance and documentary portrait records support the memorial relation without identity inference

Residual risks:

- Contemporary source fragments may remain recognisable in a completed output
- Viewers may overstate what the image process knows about identity

Review triggers:

- A distinct forensic project with lawful authority and a separate repository
- Documented participation by people with standing
- A changed artistic purpose

## full-context-contemporary-person-reveal

Status: `refused`  
Access: `public`  
Scope: `todos-somos-familiares`

**Available operation:** Display of the full contemporary source photograph during fragment emergence

**Relation refused:** A contemporary source person presented as a represented subject of the memorial

Decision basis:

- Purpose: Use the contemporary social body as dispersed visual material while keeping the disappeared person as the represented subject
- Mandate: Public availability is not treated as blanket consent for contextual exposure
- Evidence: The canonical process video can show accumulating reviewed fragments without revealing the surrounding source photograph

Residual risks:

- A reviewed face fragment may still be recognisable
- Repeated or adjacent fragments may imply more of a source face than intended

Review triggers:

- Explicit permission from the depicted person
- A separately reviewed manifestation whose purpose requires contextual display
- A changed source-person relation within the artwork

## anticipatory-traversal-assembly

Status: `refused`  
Access: `public`  
Scope: `seguimos-buscando`

**Available operation:** Use of traversal frames before the recorded encounter that made them available

**Relation refused:** Search represented as a decorative sequence over an image assembled with future knowledge

Decision basis:

- Purpose: Make continuing search and situated encounter constitute the portrait over time
- Mandate: The artwork claims a temporal search relation, not omniscient access to a completed route corpus
- Evidence: Placement histories record each source-frame encounter index and can be evaluated before release

Residual risks:

- A completed segment may still imply political closure
- Route and provider coverage shape what can be encountered

Review triggers:

- A manifestation that explicitly abandons traversal as its temporal operation
- A new live-input architecture with an equivalent causality guarantee
- A changed artistic purpose
